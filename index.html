Our Sourdough Craft

Built on Wild Time

  1. 1

    Milling & Autolyse

    We blend stone-ground organic flours with water, letting them rest to naturally develop protein bonds and extract depth before adding salt or yeast.

  2. 2

    Gentle Folds

    We build structure and strength without rough mechanical mixing. Hand-folding every bulk bin throughout the warm morning rise.

  3. 3

    24-Hour Cold Sleep

    Each loaf rests overnight in cold baskets. This slow fermentation breaks down heavy proteins, creating deep flavors and our signatures sourdough tang.
