Step by Step: How We Bake

We skip commercial shortcuts entirely. Every single loaf of sourdough that leaves our oven is hand-crafted over two sunrises using timeless, traditional methods.

01

Stoneground Flour & Starter

We partner with regional mills to source stoneground, organic heirloom wheat. Each batch begins by feeding our vibrant, mature wild starter, which has been nurtured daily for over five years to cultivate its rich flavor profile.

02

36-Hour Slow Ferment

Patience is our primary ingredient. We stretch and fold our dough by hand, then allow it to ferment slowly in a cold climate over 36 hours. This natural breakdown of starches creates bread that is extraordinarily easy on the digestion.

03

Baked at Dawn

At 4:00 AM, our bakers load the rested loaves into our stone-sole deck oven. The combination of high radiant heat and steam yields a rich, dark golden caramelized crust and a moist, airy, beautifully textured crumb.
